DataMiner Contributor Terms — Public Dataset with Game Process Audio
Version: 2026-07-29-audio-public

By registering with an invite code and checking the consent box, you confirm that:

1. You are an authorized DataMiner data contributor.

2. CAPTURED DATA. Your device captures your own gameplay video and input, including
   keyboard and mouse events, timestamps, a device fingerprint, and derived
   annotations, indices, and statistics. When audio capture is enabled, the device
   also records audio emitted by the target game's process tree, including game
   music, sound effects, and in-game dialogue. It does not intentionally record your
   microphone, other applications, or system-wide audio.

3. UPLOAD AND RESEARCH USE. You authorize DataMiner to upload, store, process, and
   analyze the data described in clause 2 for model-training and related research.

4. PUBLIC RELEASE. You also authorize DataMiner to include the data described in
   clause 2, including captured game-process audio, in a PUBLIC dataset repository
   on Hugging Face (huggingface.co/DiogenesLab). Once published, it can be downloaded
   worldwide and may be redistributed and used by third parties under the applicable
   dataset terms. This is not an internal-only research use.

5. RIGHTS NOTICE. The contributor-authored input and annotation layer is released
   under CC-BY-NC-4.0. Copyright in the game's own imagery and audio remains with the
   game's publisher or other applicable rights holders; the dataset does not claim,
   relicense, or sublicense those underlying game assets.

6. CLEAN CAPTURE AND NO PERSONAL DATA. You capture only your own single-player,
   cooperative, or bot matches on a clean account. You do not capture real
   multiplayer sessions or any other person's identifiers, voice, or chat. You keep
   microphone capture disabled and do not route third-party voice, music, browser,
   notification, or other application audio through the target game process. No real
   names, email addresses, or other personal data appear on screen or in captured
   audio. Client behaviour telemetry is uploaded for diagnostics only and remains
   limited to the desensitization allowlist.

7. WITHDRAWAL. You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal purges your captured
   data, including audio, and telemetry from DataMiner systems and excludes it from
   future dataset releases. However, because a published dataset is public and may
   already have been downloaded, withdrawal cannot recall copies distributed before
   you withdrew.
