DataMiner Contributor Terms — Teaser Public Dataset
Version: 2026-07-25-teaser

By registering with an invite code and checking the consent box, you confirm that:

1. You are an authorized DataMiner data contributor.

2. CAPTURED DATA. Your device captures your own gameplay video and input, including
   keyboard and mouse events, timestamps, a device fingerprint, and derived
   annotations, indices, and statistics. When audio capture is enabled, the device
   also records audio emitted by the target game's process tree, including game
   music, sound effects, and in-game dialogue. It does not intentionally record your
   microphone, other applications, or system-wide audio.

3. DEPTH CAPTURE AND IN-PROCESS INSTRUMENTATION. When depth capture is enabled, the
   DataMiner client loads an instrumentation component into the target game's process
   in order to read the depth buffer the game's graphics pipeline has already
   produced, together with the camera projection and pose parameters used to render
   each frame. This component reads rendering state only. It does not modify the
   game, does not alter gameplay or its outcome, does not read the memory of any
   other application, and does not transmit anything except the depth frames,
   projection and pose values, and their indices. Depth capture runs only on titles
   DataMiner has enabled for it, and never on a title that uses kernel-level
   anti-cheat.

4. UPLOAD AND RESEARCH USE. You authorize DataMiner to upload, store, process, and
   analyze the data described in clauses 2 and 3 for model-training and related
   research.

5. PUBLIC RELEASE. You also authorize DataMiner to include the data described in
   clauses 2 and 3, including captured game-process audio and depth frames, in a
   PUBLIC dataset repository on Hugging Face (huggingface.co/DiogenesLab). Once
   published, it can be downloaded worldwide and may be redistributed and used by
   third parties under the applicable dataset terms. This is not an internal-only
   research use.

6. RIGHTS NOTICE. The contributor-authored input and annotation layer is released
   under CC-BY-NC-4.0. Copyright in the game's own imagery, audio, and rendered
   geometry remains with the game's publisher or other applicable rights holders; the
   dataset does not claim, relicense, or sublicense those underlying game assets.

7. CLEAN CAPTURE AND NO PERSONAL DATA. You capture only your own single-player,
   cooperative, or bot matches on a clean account. You do not capture real
   multiplayer sessions or any other person's identifiers, voice, or chat. You keep
   microphone capture disabled and do not route third-party voice, music, browser,
   notification, or other application audio through the target game process. No real
   names, email addresses, or other personal data appear on screen or in captured
   audio. Client behaviour telemetry is uploaded for diagnostics only and remains
   limited to the desensitization allowlist.

8. WITHDRAWAL. You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal purges your captured
   data, including audio and depth, and telemetry from DataMiner systems and excludes
   it from future dataset releases. However, because a published dataset is public
   and may already have been downloaded, withdrawal cannot recall copies distributed
   before you withdrew.
